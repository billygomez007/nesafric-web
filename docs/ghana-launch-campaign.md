# Ghana Launch Campaign — Content Reference

Reusable campaign copy for the Umo Afric Ghana market launch. This is content, not code — reference
it when producing social posts, outreach messages, or a designed flyer/video. Keep it in sync with
`/ghana`, `/for-professionals`, `/for-developers` and `/for-property-owners` if the campaign
positioning changes.

**Guardrails that apply to every asset below:**
- Never claim live AI telephone answering. AI Voice Receptionist is "Coming soon."
- Never claim Umo Afric previously operated in other African countries.
- No fake testimonials, customer counts, press logos, or countdown/scarcity claims.
- Marketplace Professional is free during the Ghana launch — say so plainly, without hype punctuation.

## Central campaign line

> You handle the deal. Umo Afric handles the work around it.

## Positioning summary

Umo Afric is now live in Ghana: an intelligent real-estate operating and marketplace platform.
Property owners, landlords, property managers and developers can run their portfolio; agents,
brokers, brokerages, real estate companies and developers can market listings, manage leads and
grow their business — with AI built in. Marketplace Professional access is free during the Ghana
launch.

## D. Short video — 20–30 second storyboard/script

**Scenario:** An agent is mid-viewing with a client at a property. Around them, unrelated to the
viewing, their business keeps moving.

| Time | Visual | On-screen / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Agent walks a client through a property, gesturing at a room. Calm, confident. | (no VO yet — just ambient scene-setting) |
| 3–8s | Cut to phone/laptop screen (their Umo Afric dashboard): a notification appears — "New enquiry received." | VO: "While you're closing one deal…" |
| 8–13s | Screen: a second notification — "Prospect asked about availability." Third: "Viewing requested." Fourth: "Asked for an alternative property." Notifications stack cleanly, organised, not chaotic. | VO: "…your business keeps moving." |
| 13–18s | Quick cut back to the agent, still fully present with their client — unbothered, not on their phone. | VO: "Umo Afric organises the activity." |
| 18–22s | Screen: the lead pipeline view, enquiries now sorted into an organised list with statuses. | (no VO — let the UI read) |
| 22–27s | Agent shakes the client's hand — deal moment. Cut to black. | Text on screen: **YOU HANDLE THE DEAL.** **UMO AFRIC HANDLES THE WORK AROUND IT.** |
| 27–30s | Umo Afric wordmark. | Text on screen: **NOW LIVE IN GHANA. JOIN FREE.** |

**Notes for production:**
- Do not depict a phone call being answered by AI — no live voice AI in this cut. Notifications are
  text/digital enquiries only.
- Keep the UI screens genuine product screenshots (lead inbox, notifications) rather than
  invented mockups, once available from design.
- No stock "smiling agent" clichés — cast a real, low-key, professional tone throughout.

## Digital flyer — content specification

For a designer to produce as an image asset. Not to be rendered as an HTML page in-product.

**Design direction:** premium black/white/graphite/neutral, consistent with the Umo Afric brand —
strong typography, confident whitespace, no gold/luxury clichés, no stock photography, no map-of-
Africa or flag iconography.

**Content hierarchy (top to bottom):**

1. **NOW LIVE IN GHANA** (eyebrow / kicker)
2. **The new way to run real estate has arrived.** (headline)
3. **You handle the deal.** / **Umo Afric handles the work around it.** (sub-headline, two lines)
4. Capability row (short, single words/phrases, not a paragraph):
   - LIST
   - MANAGE LEADS
   - COORDINATE VIEWINGS
   - BUILD YOUR TEAM
   - USE AI
   - GROW YOUR BUSINESS
5. **FREE FOR MARKETPLACE PROFESSIONALS DURING LAUNCH** (offer line)
6. QR code + CTA: **Join Umo Afric** (links to `/register` or `/ghana`)

**Copy notes:** no countdown, no "!!!"-style urgency, no invented statistics. The QR code should
point to `/ghana` for print/outdoor placements (gives context before registering) or directly to
`/register` for agent-to-agent handoffs where context is already established.
